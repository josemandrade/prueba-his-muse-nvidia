# Decisions - School Vision Screening Use Case

## 2026-11-19: Screening Test and Thresholds
- Test: Snellen chart at 6m, tumbling E for non-readers, Lea symbols for pre-readers
- Thresholds: Refer if ≤6/12 in either eye, or ≥2 line difference between eyes
- Pass: ≥6/9 in each eye
- Paper form fields documented

## 2026-11-19: Referral Process
- Referral: paper letter printed, given to parent, copy in school file
- Referral carries: child name, DOB, school/grade, screening date, acuity per eye, test used, reason, screener name/contact
- No tracking of whether child was seen
- No feedback from ophthalmologist to school
- Parent is only channel back

## 2026-11-19: Ophthalmologist Output
- Diagnosis: myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism, amblyopia, etc.
- Refractive prescription when glasses needed
- Follow-up plan: review interval, treatment (e.g., patching for amblyopia)
- Outcome: seen / did not attend
- Flow back needed: diagnosis + prescription summary to school nurse; full assessment to primary care team
- Today: primary care receives nothing directly

## 2026-11-19: Parent Notification and Consent
- Notification: paper letter sent home with child
- Pass: short result letter
- Refer: referral letter + instructions to book ophthalmology
- Consent: opt-out at start of school year, no signed consent per child
- No legal mandate for reporting
- Program runs under school health program

## 2026-11-19: Data Rules
- Nurse holds screening record
- Teachers see only accommodation (glasses, front seat), never acuity values
- Records kept for school year, then archived under school records policy

## 2026-11-19: Process Expert Review Corrections (Q1-Q5)
- Q1 (Absent child): Re-screen within 2 weeks same school year. If still absent, record as missed, notify parent. Do not refer to primary care for absence alone.
- Q2 (Parent declines referral): Primary care still receives screening result; decline recorded with reason. Referral not sent.
- Q3 (Turnaround): 4 weeks expected from referral to assessment return. If no return in 4 weeks, nurse contacts parent, re-issues referral if lost.
- Q4 (Incomplete screening): Record as incomplete with reason. Re-screen within 2 weeks. If still incomplete after two attempts, refer directly to ophthalmology with partial result noted.
- Q5 (DNA): Nurse contacts parent, offers one re-referral with new appointment. If child does not attend twice, close as DNA and notify primary care.
- Referral letter copy: retained with screening record for school year, then archived together.
- Teacher accommodation notice: generated by nurse from ophthalmology feedback when received.
